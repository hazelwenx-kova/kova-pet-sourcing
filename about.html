KOVA is an independent sourcing service based in Shenzhen, China. We help small and medium-sized pet brands, importers and retailers explore manufacturer options and coordinate product development.

Our primary focus is cat products — from comfortable beds and scratching furniture to everyday feeding and play accessories. Selected dog categories complement that focus.

We work with clients in Europe and North America, while remaining open to projects from Australia and other international markets.

Our role is to help connect your requirements with supplier conversations: researching options, comparing quotations, following up on samples and coordinating production details. We do not own factories or manufacture products.
