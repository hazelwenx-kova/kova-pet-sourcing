SOURCING SERVICES

A closer connection
to the details.

Practical sourcing support for cat and dog products: identify manufacturers, discuss materials and customization, and coordinate development and production.

01

Supplier Research & Shortlisting

Research potential manufacturers for cat and dog products based on your category, materials, design and target-market requirements. We help you compare suitability before moving forward.

02

Supplier Communication & Quotation Comparison

Clarify specifications with suppliers and compare quotation details, including scope, packaging and stated lead times.

03

Product Customization & OEM/ODM Coordination

Coordinate discussions around fabric or material choices, dimensions, colorways, hardware, branding and packaging for cat and dog products. Feasibility, sample approval and ownership terms are agreed with the supplier.

04

Sample Development & Follow-up

Organize sample requests, track progress and relay your feedback so specifications can be refined before an order.

05

Factory Visits & Production Coordination

Arrange visits where appropriate and follow up on agreed production milestones. Scope and availability are confirmed for each project.

06

Quality Issue Follow-up

Communicate reported issues, gather supplier responses and coordinate next steps. Specialist testing or independent inspection can be discussed separately.

07

Order & Shipment Coordination

Follow up on order details, packing information and delivery arrangements with suppliers and your chosen logistics partners.

A clear scope, from the start.

KOVA is an independent sourcing and coordination service, not a manufacturer or certification body. Scope, fees and responsibilities are agreed before work begins. Specialist inspections or testing require a separately agreed provider; product safety and import obligations should be confirmed for your target market.
